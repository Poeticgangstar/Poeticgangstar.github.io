Clipper Automation

A private tool that turns Creative Commons videos into short vertical clips for one YouTube channel.

Uses YouTube API Services · Privacy policy · Terms of service · YouTube Terms of Service

What it does

Clipper Automation is a personal application run by one person on their own computer. It publishes short clips to that person's own YouTube channel, about crypto, hip hop and technology. It is not a public service, and nobody else signs in to it.

  1. Finds videos. It uses the YouTube Data API to search for videos that their creators published under the Creative Commons Attribution licence (CC BY). It downloads only those videos, or videos from channels that gave permission.
  2. Asks the owner. Each video is sent to the owner for approval before anything is made from it.
  3. Makes clips. It cuts approved videos into 9:16 clips of about 30 seconds, adds captions and mutes bad language.
  4. Uploads. It uploads the clips to the owner's channel through the official YouTube Data API. Every description credits the original creator and links to the source video.

How it uses Google and YouTube

The owner signs in once with Google and grants a single permission, youtube.upload, so the app can upload videos to their own channel. The app does not read, change or delete anything else on the account. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
